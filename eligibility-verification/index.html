Coverage before the visit

Eligibility Verification

Coverage, copay, deductible, and authorization checked before the patient is seen, and written where the coder can read it.

Request an estimateTalk to the studio

The problem

Why this work usually stalls.

A claim denied for inactive coverage was knowable the day before the visit.

What this desk takes on

The queue, named.

The work is staffed as its own desk, with a written handoff to the next one.

Before the patient is seen

Coverage is checked ahead of the visit, and the check is dated.

  • Active coverage confirmed
  • The date of the check on the chart

Copay, deductible, and auth

What the patient owes, and whether an authorization is required, is written for the front desk.

  • A number the desk can quote
  • Auth requested when it is required

A record the coder can use

The plan name and the limitation stay on the encounter.

  • Plan name on the chart
  • The limitation written down

Exceptions before the day starts

Inactive coverage and missing referrals are a morning list.

  • A list for the front desk
  • Time to tell the patient

On the book

What changes when the queue has an owner.

These hold for a clinic group and for a billing company.

Coverage checked in time

The answer exists before the patient arrives.

Inactive plans are a morning list, not a denial weeks later.

Auth requested when it is required

The rule is read before the service, not after.

The coder can see what was confirmed.

The desk can quote the number

Copay and deductible are written where staff stand.

The patient hears the figure before the visit, not on a later statement.
