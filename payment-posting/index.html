Payment Posting

ERA and paper EOB posting, with underpayments and patient payments kept apart from contractual write-offs.

Request an estimateTalk to the studio

The problem

Why this work usually stalls.

A payment posted to the wrong visit makes the book look collected and the patient look overdue.

What this desk takes on

The queue, named.

The work is staffed as its own desk, with a written handoff to the next one.

ERA and paper, both

Electronic remits and paper EOBs are posted to the visit they belong to.

  • The payment matches the claim
  • Unmatched items stay visible

Underpayments set aside

A short pay is marked for appeal. It is not quietly written off.

  • Contractual write-offs named
  • Short pays kept separate

Patient money, separate

Copays and patient payments are not mixed into the insurance line.

  • Who paid is recorded
  • The balance left is honest

A day that closes

Posting finishes against a batch, so tomorrow does not open on yesterday's pile.

  • Batches with a total
  • Exceptions listed before close

On the book

What changes when the queue has an owner.

A payment on the right visit

Short pays kept visible

A batch that closes
