AR Calling

Payer and patient calls on aged accounts, with a note that says what was promised and when to call again.

Request an estimateTalk to the studio

The problem

Why this work usually stalls.

An account without a next date is not being worked. It is waiting for someone to remember it.

What this desk takes on

The queue, named.

The work is staffed as its own desk, with a written handoff to the next one.

Aged accounts with a next date

Balances are called in age order, and every call ends with a day to try again.

  • The promise is written down
  • A date, not a vague follow-up

Payer calls with the claim in hand

The caller can see the codes, the denial, and what was already sent.

  • The claim number on the note
  • The missing document named

Patient balances, plainly

Statements and calls explain what insurance paid and what is still owed.

  • A balance a patient can read
  • A path to pay or to dispute

A queue that does not go quiet

The list shows who is next, including the calls that were awkward last time.

  • An owner for the day's list
  • Silence older than the rule is visible

On the book

What changes when the queue has an owner.

These hold for a clinic group and for a billing company.

A note after every call

The account carries what was said.

The next person continues without asking the patient to start again.

Promises with dates

A payer saying they will look at it is not a status.

Each open balance has a next action and a day.

The oldest money first

Age is the order of the work.

You can see which payers are holding the book.
